Extending the Life of Your Epoxy Jewelry

To keep your beloved, sparkling epoxy jewelry looking its best for years to come, there are a few things you should keep in mind. The secret to enjoying these handcrafted pieces for many more years on your special occasions lies in some simple care tips. How should you store and clean your epoxy jewelry? How should you wear it? Let’s take a closer look together.

Be sure to apply all products—such as lotions, creams, hairspray, and perfume—in advance and wait a while before putting on your jewelry. This will help prevent staining. Direct and prolonged exposure to sunlight can also cause your jewelry to discolor. After wearing your jewelry, be sure to store it in the soft cases we provide. Avoid wearing them in hot environments like saunas or keeping them near heat-emitting objects. This will help prevent potential melting. Epoxy jewelry can get scratched, broken, or bent if dropped or subjected to heavy pressure.

You can use the microfiber polishing cloth included in the box to remove scratches from your jewelry. If the scratches are severe, you can bring your jewelry to our service center or request that it be picked up from your location. Our team will return them to you polished. You can also use the same cloth for drying after washing. You can wash the cloths by hand or in the machine on a short cycle at a low temperature. Use only warm water and soap to clean your jewelry. You can purchase all the cleaning and storage products you need from our website.
